# Centenarian Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Centenarian is a habit tracker for healthy ageing. This policy explains what
the app does with your information.

**The short version: everything you enter stays on your iPhone. Centenarian
has no accounts and no servers, and we never receive your data.**

## What the app stores, and where

The app keeps the following on your device only:

- the habits you track and the days you complete them
- your progress in challenges, lessons, quizzes and protocols
- items you bookmark and studies you download
- your reminder time
- your date of birth and biological sex, if you choose to enter them (they are
  used to estimate a "lifestyle age" and to pick the right reference ranges)
- recent readings from Apple Health, if you connect it (see below)

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and we could
not hand it over if asked, because we do not have it.

## Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional. If you allow it, Centenarian reads these
six types of data: steps, sleep, exercise minutes, resting heart rate, heart
rate variability and VO₂ max.

- The app only reads this data. It never writes to Apple Health.
- Readings are used on your device to tick off matching habits automatically
  and to explain what your numbers mean.
- Readings are never shared, sold, or used for advertising or marketing.
- The copy of your readings that the app keeps is excluded from iCloud and
  computer backups.

You can withdraw access at any time in the Health app, under Sharing → Apps →
Centenarian, or in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

## What we do not do

- no accounts or sign-in
- no advertising
- no analytics or usage tracking
- no tracking across other apps or websites
- no selling or sharing of personal information

## When the app uses the internet

Centenarian works offline. It connects to the internet only when you ask it to:

- **Opening a talk, podcast, video or study** takes you to the publisher's own
  website (for example TED or Europe PMC).
- **Downloading a study** fetches the PDF directly from its publisher.

In both cases the publisher's site receives the same technical information any
website does, such as your IP address, and its own privacy policy applies.
Centenarian sends none of your habit or health information with these requests.

## Sharing

If you use the share button on a progress card, the image is passed to the app
you choose (Messages, for example). Nothing is shared unless you do this.

## Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, Apple includes
the app's habit and progress data in that backup, under your agreement with
Apple. Your cached Apple Health readings are excluded.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings → Reset all data** inside the app erases every habit, all history,
  cached readings and progress.
- Deleting the app from your iPhone removes everything it stored.

Because we hold no copy, there is nothing further to delete on our side.

## Children

Centenarian is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
The app collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that send data off your device, such as
optional accounts or community leaderboards, this policy will be updated
before those features are released, and the app will ask for your consent
before anything is shared.
